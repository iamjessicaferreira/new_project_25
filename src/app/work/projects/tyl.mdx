## About TYL

TYL is a digital platform for Ukrainian funds that accelerates and increases aid to Ukraine's defenders. The platform centralizes accounting, warehouse management, fundraising, and volunteer coordination — all in one place. Built by volunteers, for volunteers.

## My Role

As a **Frontend Developer**, I contributed to improving the platform's user interface, working on front-end enhancements, translations, and building various UI components.
