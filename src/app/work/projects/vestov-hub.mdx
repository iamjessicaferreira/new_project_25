## About Vestov Hub

Vestov Hub is an employee management and service portal designed to streamline team operations and internal workflows.

## My Role

As a **Frontend Developer**, I built several features across the platform, including asynchronous features that communicate with the back-end for:

- **Tracking Monitoring**: Real-time tracking and monitoring capabilities
- **Shift Management**: Shift scheduling and monitoring tools
- **Request Handling**: Internal request and approval workflows
- **Slack Integration**: Real-time notifications and integrations with Slack for seamless team communication
