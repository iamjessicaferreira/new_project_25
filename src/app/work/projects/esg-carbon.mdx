## About ESG Carbon

ESG Carbon is a platform designed to help companies calculate, track, and manage their carbon emissions with clarity and speed. The platform offers:

- **Data Collection**: Configure ESG metrics organized by areas, business units, and relevant categories, with API integration capabilities
- **Automated Calculations**: Sophisticated calculation engine for carbon emissions processing
- **Real-time Dashboards**: Consolidated information with analytics to compare performance across periods and business units
- **Reporting**: Generate reports aligned with standards such as GRI, SASB, IFRS S1/S2, and ISE, with built-in data auditing capabilities
- **Decarbonization**: Integrated goal and initiative management with centralized tracking of responsibilities, deadlines, and status

## My Role

As a **Fullstack Developer**, I worked across both the backend and frontend:

- **Backend**: Implemented and automated the carbon emissions calculation engine, developing complex formulas for accurate emissions processing
- **Frontend**: Built interactive charts, dashboards, and other UI components focused on platform usability and data visualization
