## About JOTA

JOTA.info is an independent press outlet and digital media startup specializing in the coverage of Brazil's Judiciary, Supreme Courts, National Congress, and regulatory agencies. Elected the world's best media startup in 2019, JOTA focuses on technical analysis, political backstage insights, and the legal impact on business, serving as a reference portal for decision-makers.

## My Role

As a **Fullstack Developer**, I contributed to multiple areas of the platform:

- **Design System & Frontend**: Worked on building and maintaining the Design System, developing reusable UI components for the front-end
- **Backend Automation**: Implemented process automation on the back-end using Python and Django
