# What moving from an Anti-AI environment to an AI-First team taught me about being a developer

The conversation around AI in software development has become impossible to ignore. But what happens when you experience both extremes: a team that actively resists AI and one that fully embraces it?

That's exactly what happened to me, and the lessons I learned along the way completely reshaped how I think about my role as a developer.

In my previous environment, AI tools were seen as a threat, something that would replace developers or make us lazy. The resistance was strong, and using AI-assisted tools was discouraged. Then I moved to a team where AI wasn't just accepted, it was a core part of the workflow.

The contrast was eye-opening. It wasn't about AI replacing anyone, it was about amplifying what we could do. The developers who thrived weren't the ones who could memorize syntax or write boilerplate the fastest. They were the ones who could think critically, ask the right questions, and use every tool at their disposal to solve real problems.

This experience taught me that being a great developer isn't about the tools you use or refuse to use, it's about your ability to adapt, learn, and deliver value.
